# Pump holder distribution and depth-one nested creation recovery

This checkpoint integrates two previously isolated recoveries into frozen main41: `distribute_fee_to_holders`, and the depth-one normal SOL-root branch of `create_v2`. The resulting source has 42 recovered handlers and four partial paths. It is not a complete historical-source reconstruction or a deployable replacement.

Only three Rust paths changed: the new `holder_distribution.rs`, its module/export/handler entries in `lib.rs`, and the depth-one changes in `create_v2.rs`. The Cargo manifest, lockfile, shared Global and BondingCurve layouts, trade implementations and all other source bytes were preserved. The earlier combined extension preflight was not used. The source archives and exact delta are retained in `repro/pump/holder-nested-fortytwo/`.

Holder distribution uses the configured, normally signed claim authority. The recovered path performs native or token payouts, uses actual Token/Token22/ATA CPIs, and preserves the tested recipient-account, source-account, rent-floor and event behavior. Its 649 differential cases contain 286 successful original executions and 363 rejections; its 40 Surfpool comparisons contain 26 successes and 14 rejections. Nine preexisting Token22 extension fixtures are confined to this holder-distribution instruction; no trade-fee or extension recovery was undertaken.

Nested creation starts from a normal root coin genuinely created by the original program with ordinary test-owned signatures. It validates the parent relationship and supported depth, computes the initial quote reserves, and persists/emits the resulting depth. The 48 focused differential cases and 48 Surfpool comparisons each include 33 successful original child executions and 15 rejections. These fixtures contain no quote-extension inputs. Mayhem roots, migrated parents and deeper nested chains remain unsupported or partial.

The clean pinned v1.44 main and scratch builds are whole-file identical: 1,405,520 bytes, SHA-256 `c07e67b055c87da881b33c9e07bddf913d5b0beffa1cfe52169ba1bc3f6d2bbf`. The complete 33-suite main41 regression was rerun on those bytes (7,957 comparisons), plus the two focused suites (697), giving 8,654 comparisons across 35 suites. All pass. All 26 prior Surfpool runners were freshly rerun (936 comparisons), plus the two focused runners (88), giving 1,024 comparisons across 28 runners and 2,048 independently verified signed transactions. None of the older isolated candidates' receipts were reused to accept this executable.

The package binds 31 compiler inputs, 86 maintained test/build-helper files, 27 captured runtime/fixture inputs and the recorded orchestration. It includes the nested recording helper and its transitive Fees imports, as well as the holder fixture's captured Global input. Raw transactions, state/error/event/CPI evidence, actual commands, build logs, source snapshots, original frozen handoff manifests and the main/scratch identity check are retained. Comparisons preserve each runner's documented limits, including recognized event timestamp normalization. The read-only independent verifier checks saved hashes and signatures without executing transactions.

The current `sell_v2` quote-transfer-fee minimum-output check and fee-event fields retain a confirmed mismatch with the captured original; passing saved suites do not cover that boundary. No live mutation, privileged-signer bypass, executable patching, source padding or compiler-flag search was used.
